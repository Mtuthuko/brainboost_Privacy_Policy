1. Who We Are

Brain Boost Quiz App ("we," "us," or "our") is operated as an independent mobile application available on the Google Play Store. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy or our practices, contact us at support@brainboost.app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on how you use the app.

Data TypeWhat We CollectWhy
Account Data Email address, display name (if you create an account) To save your progress and sync across devices
Game Progress XP points, level completions, quiz scores, streaks To provide the core app experience
Purchase Data Subscription status (Pro / Free). We do NOT store card details. To unlock premium features
Device Data Device type, OS version, app version, crash logs To diagnose bugs and improve performance
Ad Data Advertising ID (free users only), ad interaction data To serve relevant advertisements
Usage Analytics Screen views, feature usage (anonymised) To understand how the app is used and improve it

We do not collect: precise location, contacts, microphone, camera, or any sensitive personal data.

3. Advertising (Free Users)

Brain Boost is free to play with optional advertisements. Free users will see banner ads and interstitial ads between quiz rounds. These ads are served by third-party advertising networks including Google AdMob.

  • Ad networks may use your Advertising ID to serve relevant ads.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads in your Google account settings.
  • Pro subscribers see zero ads. Upgrading to Pro immediately removes all advertising.

Our advertising partners comply with applicable privacy laws. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Brain Boost offers a Pro subscription ($4.99/month) processed entirely through the Google Play billing system.

  • We never see or store your payment card details — these are handled securely by Google.
  • We receive only your subscription status (active/inactive) from Google.
  • Subscriptions auto-renew monthly unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through the Google Play Store app.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the app
  • Save and sync your game progress across sessions
  • Process subscription purchases and unlock premium features
  • Show advertisements to free users
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve the app (anonymised data only)
  • Send important service updates (e.g. billing issues, app updates)
  • Diagnose and fix bugs and crashes

We do not use your data for: selling to third parties, building profiles for non-advertising purposes, or any automated decision-making that significantly affects you.

6. Data Sharing

We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use trusted third-party services (e.g. Firebase for analytics, AdMob for ads, RevenueCat for subscriptions) that process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.
  • Business transfer: If the app is acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the new owner subject to the same privacy protections.

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Anonymised, aggregated analytics data (which cannot identify you) may be retained indefinitely for product improvement purposes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Opt-out of ads: Opt out of personalised advertising via your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@brainboost.app and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Brain Boost Quiz App is rated "Everyone" on Google Play and is suitable for all ages. However, the app is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without parental consent.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at support@brainboost.app and we will delete the data promptly.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), secure cloud infrastructure, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
